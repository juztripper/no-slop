# Community code of conduct

NO SLOP welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Work together with care

- Critique behavior, code, and evidence rather than the person behind them. Disagreement and unfamiliar writing styles are not evidence of low quality.
- Give useful feedback, make room for questions, and respect people's time and boundaries.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, use sexualized language or imagery, or repeatedly disrupt discussions.
- Do not publish someone else's private information, credentials, private feed, or correspondence without permission.
- Follow the same standards when discussing creators whose content appears in an example. Use sanitized, consented examples and avoid directing harassment at anyone.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on an offending issue, pull request, or comment for a private report to GitHub. For a moderation request that is safe to discuss publicly, mention [@juztripper](https://github.com/juztripper) in the relevant project conversation. Do not repeat personal information or harmful material in a public report.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

The project maintainer, [@juztripper](https://github.com/juztripper), may ask for a correction, edit or remove harmful content, close or lock a disruptive conversation, or restrict participation. The response should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Retaliation against someone raising a concern is unacceptable.

Maintainers follow these standards too. Good-faith reports and honest mistakes should be handled respectfully; repeated or serious harm may require an immediate restriction.
